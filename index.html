RULES


Bloodgate: The Wildwood Deception


Rules of Combat

Welcome, warrior! Prepare yourself for strategic card battles against demonic forces. Master your deck, choose your class wisely, and reclaim the Wildwood from darkness!


Game Objective

Reduce your opponent's health to 0 by playing creatures and attacking strategically. Each player starts with 30 health.


How to Play

  • Power System: Each turn, you gain 1 power (maximum = turn number). Mages start with +2 power bonus.
  • Drawing Cards: Draw 1 card at the start of each turn from your deck.
  • Playing Cards: Click cards in your hand to play them. Each card costs power (shown in top-left corner).
  • Field Limit: Maximum 4 cards can be on your field at once.
  • Attacking: Click your creatures to select them, then click enemy creatures or the enemy avatar to attack.
  • Card Stats: Each card has Cost (mana), Attack (damage dealt), and Defense (health).
  • Combat: When creatures battle, they deal their attack damage to each other's defense. Cards with 0 defense are destroyed.

Class Abilities

  • Barbarian: Breakthrough - Excess attack damage carries over to enemy health.
  • Mage: Arcane Power - Start each turn with +2 power.
  • Rogue: Initiative - Your cards can attack immediately when played.

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: Enemy has normal power progression.
  • Medium: Enemy starts with +2 power each turn.
  • Hard: Enemy starts with +4 power each turn.
  • Diabolical: Enemy starts with +6 power each turn. Good luck!

The Lore of Bloodgate


The Fall of the Wildwood

Long ago, the realm of Wildwood flourished under the protection of the Elder Council - powerful mages, noble warriors, and cunning rogues who maintained the ancient Bloodgate, a mystical barrier separating the mortal realm from the Shadowlands.


But treachery came from within. The court wizard Malachar, consumed by forbidden knowledge and dark ambition, sought to breach the Bloodgate and claim dominion over the demonic legions beyond. In a night of fire and blood, he shattered the ancient seals, unleashing a horde of nightmares upon the unsuspecting world.


The Wildwood Deception

The demons came not as mindless beasts, but as cunning strategists. They took the forms of dragons, corrupted nature spirits, and twisted reflections of fallen heroes. They transformed the lush forests into haunted groves, the pristine rivers into rivers of shadow, and the peaceful villages into battlegrounds of eternal conflict.


The survivors speak of the Wildwood Deception - the demons' cruel game of hope and despair. They allow champions to rise, gathering followers and building strength, only to crush them at their peak. Each defeat feeds the Bloodgate, strengthening the demonic hold on the realm.


The Last Hope

Yet prophecy speaks of a true champion - one who can master the ancient arts of the Elder Council, wield creatures both noble and savage, and finally close the Bloodgate forever. The demons grow restless, for they sense this champion approaches.


Could it be you?


The Three Paths

The Barbarian's Path: Channel raw fury and breakthrough strikes. Warriors who walk this path believe that overwhelming force can shatter any demonic defense. Their legendary "Bloodfury Dominion" teaches that when one enemy falls, the momentum continues to the next.


The Mage's Path: Harness arcane power and summon dragons from the astral plane. Masters of the "Dragon's Wrath" deck command mighty wyrms and ancient wizards, wielding magic that grows stronger with each passing moment.


The Rogue's Path: Strike swiftly before the enemy can react. The "Legion of the Forest" favors speed over power, teaching that a blade in the shadows is worth a sword in the sun. Their initiative allows attacks that catch demons unprepared.


Choose your path wisely, champion. The fate of Wildwood rests in your hands.
